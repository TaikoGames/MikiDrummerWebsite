Lift the Anchor

Punk Rock Vancouver, BC

Lift the Anchor is a Vancouver punk rock band built on fresh starts and loud guitars. Big hooks, gang vocals and choruses made for basement singalongs — melodic punk with just enough grit to leave a mark.

Their story is one of pulling up roots: small-town beginnings and a Toronto zip code traded for the West Coast, chasing a clean slate and a scene worth showing up for. That restless, start-over energy runs through everything they write — songs about leaving, arriving, and figuring it out at full volume.

The 2025 demos Hello RPM and Aurora lay out the blueprint: fast, catchy and heart-on-sleeve, somewhere in the lineage of Sum 41, Four Year Strong and Silverstein. Catch them on a Vancouver bill — dates land on the Punk BC shows board.

Tracks: Hello RPM (Demo) · Aurora (Demo) · Cornered (Demo)

For fans of: Sum 41 · Silverstein · Four Year Strong · Papa Roach

Granite

Metal / Grunge Vancouver, BC

GRANITE is a Vancouver metal band that refuses to sit still in one genre. Equal parts crushing metal and dirt-under-the-fingernails grunge, their sound is heavy, hook-driven and hard to file — the kind of noise that hits like a boot to the chest and lingers like a bad decision.

Forged in Vancouver's underground, GRANITE deal in downtuned riffs, raw-throated vocals and a live show that leaves rooms sweat-soaked and ears ringing. It's grunge grit welded to metal weight: sludgy when it wants to be, savage when it needs to be, and always built to move a crowd.

Whether they're splitting a bill with local heavyweights or tearing up a basement show, GRANITE bring the same thing every night — real, loud, unfiltered heavy music from the Pacific Northwest. Hard to describe. Easy to feel.

Tracks: Blindsight · Skookum · Hemogoblin · Pony Up · Sour Diesel · Tiamat · Cursed · Astro Fungus · Devilbliss

Bound By None

Punk Rock Vancouver, BC

Bound By None is a Vancouver, BC punk rock band — fast, riff-driven songs with plenty of low end and hooks that stick. Short, sharp and built for a loud room.

Two Bound By None tracks stream on the player at mikidrummer.ca: No Other and On The Double. Both sit on the heavier end of Vancouver punk rock — driving tempos, thick bass and choruses that arrive without wasting your time getting there.

Tracks: No Other · On The Double

Diera

Punk Rock Barcelona, Spain

Diera is a punk rock band out of Barcelona, Spain — Spanish-language punk with speed, melody and bite, and the project that connects this Vancouver drum stool to the Catalan scene.

The Diera catalogue includes Bajo el nivel del mal, Groenlandia, La tragedia de los comunes and Sebastopol García, alongside Per mi — sung in Catalan rather than Spanish. All of them stream on the player at mikidrummer.ca.

Tracks: Bajo el nivel del mal · Groenlandia · La tragedia de los comunes · Sebastopol García · Per mi (in Catalan)

Listen on the site player

Dead Fast

Punk Rock Vancouver, BC

Dead Fast is a Vancouver, BC punk rock band — quick, melodic and built to keep a room moving. The name is the brief: get in, hit hard, get out.

Three Dead Fast tracks stream on the player at mikidrummer.ca — Skamen, Land and Sea and Star Shines Through — melodic West Coast punk that trades polish for momentum.

Tracks: Skamen · Land and Sea · Star Shines Through
