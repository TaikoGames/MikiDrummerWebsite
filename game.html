Where the notes come from

Nobody charted these songs by hand. A script reads each recording, splits the spectrum into the bands a drum kit lives in — the kick down at the bottom, the snare in the crack around 1.5 kHz to 4 kHz, the cymbals above 6 kHz — and looks for the moment energy jumps in each one. That is an onset, and an onset is a drum hit. So the notes coming down the lanes are the parts Miki actually played on the record, not somebody's transcription of them.

It is not a perfect listener, and it is not pretending to be. A bass guitar playing eighths lives in the same octave as a kick drum, so the low band gets an opinion about notes that were never played with a foot, and each lane is capped to keep a chart playable rather than literal. Twelve songs are charted so far, across Bound By None, Dead Fast, Diera, Lift the Anchor and Granite. Adding another is one workflow run.
