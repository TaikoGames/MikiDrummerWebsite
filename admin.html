Live controls

🎬 Intro video live
When ON, visitors landing on the homepage see the intro video full-screen with a close button. Turn OFF for a normal homepage. The video itself is already set in the site config — this is just the on/off.
Show for the next:
Flipping the switch (or a timer) saves straight to config.json and the site updates in about a minute — no download.
🪧 Poster live
Paste an image link to pop a poster full-screen (with a fade-in) the moment visitors arrive — a gig flyer, a release. Leave the field empty for no poster.
Auto-remove after:
Show for seconds each visit, then it fades out.
Any public image URL works (JPG, PNG, GIF — an animated GIF plays as it pops in). Clear the field and Save to turn the poster off; pick a day count to auto-remove it.
📰 News ticker live
The scrolling news bar at the top of the site. One item per block: Title, optional Description, Link, Expires: YYYY-MM-DD.
⭐ Featured video live
The big video at the top of the Videos page. Pick one you already have, or paste a new YouTube link.
🖼️ Photos live
