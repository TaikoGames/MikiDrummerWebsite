Ordering

Anything with a Buy now button goes straight to a card checkout and is paid for there. Anything with an Order button has no checkout wired up yet — tell me what you want using the form and you get a note back with the total and how to pay. Nothing is charged until you have said yes to a number.

How it actually works

  • Made to order. Nothing sits in a box waiting. Expect a few days before it ships, longer if a few orders land at once.
  • Vancouver pickup is free, and usually faster than the post.
  • Shipping across Canada at cost — these are small and light, so it is not much.
  • If it breaks, tell me. It is a printed part made in my room, not a moulding from a factory. A replacement costs me an afternoon, which is cheaper than an argument.
