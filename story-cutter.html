How to turn a video into story clips

  1. Choose the video. Anything your phone or camera records — landscape or upright.
  2. Find the best moments. Pick a length and how many clips you want. It scores every stretch of the video on how loud it is and how much it moves, takes the best ones that don't overlap, and starts each one on the nearest hit.
  3. Frame it. “Fill the screen” crops to 9:16 — slide the position to keep the singer or the kit in shot. “Whole frame + blur” keeps everything and fills the rest with a blurred copy.
  4. Adjust and save. Nudge any clip a second either way, add one from wherever the player is, then save them one at a time or all at once.

Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. The video never leaves your phone or computer.

Why does saving take as long as the clip?

Each clip is recorded as it plays, so a 15 second clip takes about 15 seconds. Keep this tab in front while it works.

What format are the clips?

1080×1920 MP4 on iPhone, Android and current Chrome, Edge and Safari. A few desktop browsers can only save WebM; if yours does, open the page on your phone instead.

How do I post them?

On a phone, “Save” opens the share sheet, so you can send a clip straight to Instagram or save it to Photos. On a computer it downloads.
