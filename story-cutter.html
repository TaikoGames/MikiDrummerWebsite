How to turn a video into story clips

  1. Choose a video. Anything your phone or camera records — landscape or upright.
  2. Wait a moment. It listens for the parts where the song goes off and makes a vertical 9:16 clip of each one, starting on the hit.
  3. Save. Save one, or save them all. On a phone that opens the share sheet, so they can go straight to Instagram or into Photos.

Want more control? Open Options to change the clip length and how many, crop or fit the frame, add a caption, or pick your own moments.

Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. The video never leaves your phone or computer.

Why does it take a minute?

Each clip is recorded as it plays, so five 15 second clips take a little over a minute. Keep this tab open while it works.

What format are the clips?

1080×1920 MP4 on iPhone, Android and current Chrome, Edge and Safari. A few desktop browsers can only save WebM; if yours does, open the page on your phone instead.

How do I post them?

On a phone, tap “Save” and pick “Save Video” to put the clip in your Photos, or pick Instagram to post it straight away. “Download the file” under each clip saves it to your Files or Downloads instead. On a computer, “Save” downloads it.

Why won’t it save from Instagram?

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook open links in their own built-in browser, which can’t save files. Tap the ⋯ menu, open the page in Safari or Chrome, and it works.
