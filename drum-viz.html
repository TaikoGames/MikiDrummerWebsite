Drum Trigger Visualiser

Your kit on screen, lighting up as you play — hit the snare, the snare flashes. Drive it from an e-kit or triggers over MIDI, or from a live audio feed. Set the background to green or transparent and drop it over drum cam footage.

Tap the canvas or press keys to test: space kick · s snare · h hats · t/y toms · f floor · c crash · r ride. Clean mode hides everything but the kit — Esc to come back.

Input

Nothing connected — keyboard and clicks still work.

Look

Pieces — tap Learn, then hit it

Layout comes from drum-setup.json — the same kit as the drum map, so editing it there moves the pieces here too. Notes default to the General MIDI drum map.

Esc to exit clean mode
