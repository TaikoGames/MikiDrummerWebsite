Press kit editor

No login. Tick what should appear, rename a song, add a photo or a video, then send the changes. Nothing here goes live on its own — Miki applies it, which is deliberate: a page that could publish without a password could be published to by anyone.

The live set

Unticking a song hides it from the kit — the recording stays where it is, nothing is deleted.

Photos

Or drop a photo here — or tap to choose one from your phone

To add your own: drop the file in the band's Drive folder, set it to anyone with the link, copy the link and paste it above. Miki turns it into a proper hosted image when he applies the change — Drive links are fine here, but they are slow and can expire, so they do not go on the live page as-is.

Videos

YouTube only, on purpose: a phone video is often a gigabyte, which the site cannot host and nobody will wait to load. Upload it to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and paste the link — any format works, youtu.be/…, watch?v=… or an embed link.
