The Stage Playbook

Two things every set needs and nobody rehearses: how we talk to each other when it's too loud to talk, and what we show up in. Pull this up before doors.

When in doubt — watch the drummer 🥁
01

Stage Signals

Hand cues to run the song mid-song — no talking required.

Structure — where the song goes next

🔁
Around Again
DOIndex finger circling in the air.
MEANSRepeat this part — one more time.
⏭️
Next
DOJab a finger forward, toward the crowd.
MEANSMove on — into the next section.
🔝
From the Top
DOTap the top of your head.
MEANSBack to the top of the song.
✊
Big Ending
DORaise a fist, then punch it down on the hit.
MEANSLast one — stop together on the stab.

Dynamics — how big it gets

🤫
Bring It Down
DOFlat palm, press it down slowly.
MEANSPull back — quieter, tighter.
📢
Lift It
DOPalm up, raise it toward the ceiling.
MEANSBuild — louder, bigger, open up.

Cues — heads up

👀
Watch Me
DOTwo fingers to your eyes, then point at them.
MEANSEyes up — a cue is coming.
🤘
You're Up
DOPoint straight at the player.
MEANSYour solo — take it.
🥁
Count It In
DOFour stick clicks, or count on fingers.
MEANSI'll bring us in — come in together.

Stop — the only one that overrides everything

✂️
Cut
DOSlice a flat hand across your throat.
MEANSDead stop — end it now, no tail.
02

Dress Code

We're one band on stage — look like it.

Always

non-negotiable
  • ✓Head-to-toe black (or close). Hides sweat, beer, and mistakes.
  • ✓Closed shoes you can stomp in — boots or beat-up sneakers.
  • ✓Something you can thrash in. Nothing that rides up or tears.
  • ✓Earplugs. They count as gear.

Show Night

nice touches
  • ✓One band tee — someone else's band, never our own.
  • ✓A layer you don't mind losing to the pit or the stage lights.
  • ✓IEMs / batteries charged, sticks and spares in the bag.
  • ✓A little stage presence — this is a show, not a rehearsal.

Never

leave it home
  • ✕Flip-flops, sandals, anything open-toed near a cymbal stand.
  • ✕Our own merch on stage — you're not a fan tonight.
  • ✕Bright white that's grey by song three.
  • ✕Loose jewelry that snags a strap, a stand, or a snare.
