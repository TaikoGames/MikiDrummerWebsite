Cymbal Designer

Draw straight onto the cymbal — engrave to cut into the metal, or paint to lay colour on top. Quarter symmetry repeats every stroke at 90°, 180° and 270°, so a single gesture becomes a design that wraps the whole cymbal. Download the result as a PNG.

Drag on the cymbal to draw. Everything happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Your design is kept on this device, so you can close the tab and come back to it.
