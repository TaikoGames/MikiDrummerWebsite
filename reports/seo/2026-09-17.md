# SEO check — 2026-09-17

First run, so there is nothing to compare against. This is the baseline every
later report diffs from.

**156 pages, 139 indexable — 25 high · 31 medium · 25 low**

Counts on their own are misleading here. A third of the "high" findings are
one problem wearing several hats, and most of the "low" ones are not worth
anyone's afternoon. What follows is sorted by whether it would change traffic,
not by what the script shouted loudest about.

---

## Fixed in this pass

**A dead ticket link on the live shows board.** One show — Sat 31 Oct at The
Astoria — had a ticket URL of `Column 1`, a spreadsheet header that leaked
into a data row on import. It rendered as a clickable show card that went
nowhere, on `punkbc.html` and two show pages. Guarded in both renderers
(`href_for()` in `build_punkbc.py` and the client-side one in `punkbc.html`,
which build the same markup by different routes): a ticket field that is not
a URL now falls back to the venue link, same as a blank one.

## Worth doing, in order

### 1. `/files/` — a whole stale copy of the site is live and indexable

Four pages: `index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `video.html`. An old
snapshot from August that never got removed.

- Three are titled **"Mirkidrummer"** — the brand name misspelled.
- `files/index.html` carries the **exact same `<title>` as the real home
  page**, so it is a duplicate competing for the brand query.
- `files/index.html` canonicalises to `/`, which at least points home. The
  other three have no canonical at all.
- None are `noindex`. None are in the sitemap, and nothing on the site links
  to them — so Google may not have found them. May.

This is the same problem as `service.html` (fixed 2026-09-17), four more times.

**Fix:** `noindex` on all four, or delete them. Ten minutes. Non-destructive
either way — nothing links to them, so nothing breaks.
**Blocked on:** Miki confirming `/files/` is genuinely dead and not serving
some purpose the directory name hints at.

### 2. Band pages outnumber city pages 106 to 8

The standing biggest opportunity, and it is a content job rather than a bug.

"Alien Boys shows" is a handful of searches a month. "Vancouver punk shows"
is a real query with real volume. The site has 106 pages chasing the first
kind and 8 chasing the second — and the 8 are the ones already earning
impressions.

**Fix:** build out city pages (Kelowna, Nanaimo, Surrey, Burnaby, Victoria
already exists) and more venue pages. The generator in
`tools/build_punkbc.py` already knows how; it is mostly a matter of deciding
which cities have enough shows to justify a page.
**Effort:** an afternoon for the first four.

### 3. `/about.html` — 78 words, no `<h1>`

The third-most-linked page on the site and it is a stub: one paragraph, no
heading, and the same Ibiza sentence that appears on the home page. A page
about a named person, with no `<h1>` carrying that name.

**Fix:** an `<h1>`, and enough real content to justify the page existing.
Same treatment as the home page and services got, in the same first-person
voice as `bands.html`.
**Effort:** an hour.

### 4. `/video.html` — 33 words, no `<h1>`

In the main nav, so it gets crawled often, and there is nothing on it to
crawl. Video pages can rank well because video results are their own surface,
but not without text saying what the videos are.

**Fix:** `<h1>`, a line per video, and `VideoObject` structured data.
**Effort:** an hour, more if the video list is long.

## Noise — findings I would ignore

Recording these so later runs do not keep re-raising them as if they were new.

- **`thin` on the tool pages** (`/bpm/`, `/drum-map.html`, `/jukebox.html`,
  `/click-track.html`, `/seal-tool.html`, `/slideshow.html`). These are
  interactive tools. The word count is low because the page *is* the tool,
  not because anything is missing. `/bpm/` at 16 words could earn traffic on
  "online metronome" with some text, but the rest are not search targets.
- **`title-long` ×9, `desc-long` ×11.** Google truncates; it does not
  penalise. Only worth touching if the important words are falling off the
  end, which they are not.
- **`h1-missing` on `/seal-tool.html` and `/slideshow.html`.** Neither is
  meant to rank. Better fixed by marking them `noindex` than by writing
  headings for them — which would also clear them off the thin list.
- **`sitemap-absent` ×14.** Most are admin or tool pages that should be
  `noindex` rather than added. Worth one pass to decide which is which.

## What this check cannot see

No Search Console access — no impressions, queries, positions or CTR. Every
judgement above is about the state of the site itself, which is the half that
is controllable but not the half that tells you whether it worked.

To close that gap, the useful things to look up by hand:

- **Performance → Queries** for `/services.html` over the next month. It was
  rewritten 2026-09-17 from 128 words to 753; new service-type queries
  appearing would confirm it landed.
- **Pages → Why pages aren't indexed**, filtered for `/files/`. If those four
  appear as indexed, item 1 goes from precautionary to urgent.
- **Sitemaps → Last read**, to confirm Google is still fetching it.

## Top three

1. Kill `/files/` — a duplicate of the home page is competing with the home page.
2. Build city pages, not band pages — that is where the search volume is.
3. Give `/about.html` and `/video.html` an `<h1>` and something to say.
